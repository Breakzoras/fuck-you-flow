# SEO research: Fuck You Flow (fuckyouflow.app)

Measured 2026-09-06. Source: **Google Keyword Planner**, Lu's Google Ads account 900-463-1048,
plan "Plan from Sept 6, 2026, 8 am". Read only, no campaign created, no money spent
(the account banner reads "None of your ads are running").

Semrush was tried first and returned nothing: every call failed with "active subscription,
but does not have enough API units". Unblock at https://www.semrush.com/mcp-access if precise
volumes are ever needed. Keyword Planner gives banded ranges rather than exact numbers,
because the account has no active spend. The bands are enough to rank the terms against
each other, which is what the decision needed.

## 1. The finding that decides the strategy

The competitor's own brand terms carry **ten times the volume** of every generic
"free dictation" term, at **low competition**, and they are **growing tenfold year on year**.

| Keyword | Avg. monthly searches | Competition | YoY change | Top of page bid (high) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **wispr flow alternative** | **1k - 10k** | Low | **+900%** | EUR 15.20 |
| **wispr flow pricing** | **1k - 10k** | Low | **+900%** | EUR 13.14 |
| **wispr flow free** | 100 - 1k | Low | **+900%** | EUR 28.15 |

The bid figures matter as a signal of commercial value: advertisers pay up to EUR 28 for a
click on "wispr flow free". That is money chasing the exact person we want.

**This corrected a claim on our own page.** The bill table said "12 to 15 dollars a month"
against our "0, forever", which reads as though they charge from the first word. They do not.
The row now reads "Free to 2,000 words a week, then 12 to 15 dollars a month", and a new row

The honest version is also the stronger one, because the weekly cap is the real pain.

Their homepage headline on the pricing page: "Speak. Write faster. Meet smarter."
No accuracy claim is published on either page.

## 5. The plan that follows

1. **A dedicated comparison page is the single highest value asset.** Target
   "wispr flow alternative", "wispr flow pricing", "wispr flow free". Low competition,
   thousands of searches a month, tenfold growth, and the buyer is already price sensitive.
2. **The English root page** carries "free dictation software for windows" plus the offline
   and local angle ("offline speech to text", "local speech to text"), all measured at
   100 - 1k with low to medium competition.
3. **The Greek page** stays clean, indexed and honest, sized to a 10 - 100 a month market.
4. **Bing** matters here more than its market share suggests: this is a Windows desktop app,
   and Bing is the default search in Windows and in Edge. Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools
   alongside Google Search Console.
5. Verification for both engines must be a **meta tag or a DNS record**. The site
   Content Security Policy allows scripts only from the site itself, so any external
   verification script is blocked silently.
